# Yulu v0.30.0

Live captions become live notes. While you record, Yulu keeps the meeting's key
points beside it instead of scrolling a line of captions, takes the notes you
type, and saves both with the recording. A recording whose live transcript is
already complete is no longer transcribed a second time.

- **Live notes panel:** a tall glass panel on the right edge of the screen
  replaces the caption strip (ADR-012). Notes are rich text: headings, lists,
  tables, and Mermaid architecture diagrams drawn on your Mac. A section that
  just changed is briefly highlighted. Collapse the panel to the breathing Yulu
  logo and click the logo to bring it back; drag the header to move it; the red
  square stops the recording.
- **Your own notes:** type a line into the panel and press Return. Typing does
  not bring Yulu to the front, so the meeting app keeps its place. Your notes
  are saved with their time even when live notes are off, and every summary
  provider reads them with the transcript.
- **Chinese or English:** choose `中` or `EN` in the panel. Switching rewrites
  the notes in that language. The default follows the interface language.
- **Glossary:** live notes use the glossary entries that match the meeting, so
  names and jargon come out the way you spell them.
- **Saved with the recording:** the reading page has a **Notes** tab next to
  **Summary** and **Transcript**. On that tab, **Copy Markdown** copies the notes with
  your own notes at the end. Your Agent gets them from MCP `recording_get`.
- **No second transcription:** when the live transcript finished cleanly, uses
  the same language and engine, and covers at least 98% of the audio, Yulu
  commits it as the transcript right away. With xAI, the audio is uploaded once.
  The transcript shows a quiet line when it came from the live transcript, and
  **Re-transcribe** still runs a full pass.
- **Caption translation retired:** bilingual and translation-only captions and
  their setting are removed. Dictation translation with `Fn+Shift` is
  unchanged.

## Turning on live notes

Live notes are off after the update and send nothing until you turn them on in
**Settings → Recording & summaries → Live notes**. Turning them on first shows
what is sent: the new transcript text, including what other people say, your
notes, and matching glossary terms, directly to xAI with response storage off.
Audio is never sent for live notes. They need a connected xAI account and work
with either audio engine. They use the fast `grok-4.20-0309-non-reasoning`
model and never switch to another model or provider.

## Updating

Use Yulu's update check, or download `yulu-macos-arm64-v0.30.0.dmg` and drag
`Yulu.app` into Applications after recording has finished. If macOS blocks the
first launch, confirm the download came from the official release page, then
use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

On first launch, a caption translation setting, if you had one, is archived
next to your config as `config.legacy-transcription.<stamp>.json` and removed
from the active file, and its old consent is deleted. Recordings, history, and
provider credentials are not changed.

## Verification scope

CI ran the web type check, unit tests, and production build, the Python tests,
and the native build, including the panel helper's self-test. The live notes
page was exercised in a browser against fictional fixture data. These checks
do not cover a real meeting on an installed `Yulu.app` or the panel on every
supported macOS version; macOS 13 remains the deployment target.
