# Yulu v0.25.0

This release improves dictation and voice questions, refreshes the recording
panel, and unifies meeting reminders and recording notifications in the native App.

- **Voice input:** press the shortcut once to start and again to finish. Hold to
  talk remains available in Settings. Early key release, repeated key events,
  cancellation, and late responses cannot start or revive an older recording.
- **Transcription:** xAI realtime audio uses improved resampling, glossary
  keyterms, and bounded startup and completion. Translation and voice questions
  can reuse the completed realtime transcript. Optional dictation cleanup uses
  the selected, authorized xAI conversation connection; a failed or slow cleanup
  preserves the original text. Local dictation remains on-device.
- **Automatic insertion:** missing Accessibility permission is detected in
  Yulu.app, with a link to the relevant macOS setting. Only confirmed insertion
  reports success. Otherwise, the recognized text remains available in the panel
  and local history, with Copy Text for recovery.
- **Voice panel:** a compact panel follows the system appearance and shows a
  segmented blue waveform that matches Yulu's colors, without a separate frame
  or background around the waveform. Finish, cancel, and recovery controls stay
  close to the recording status.
- **Voice questions:** general questions no longer depend on matching a meeting
  record. General and meeting conversations keep separate scopes; changing scope
  starts a new conversation. xAI replies appear progressively, with stale updates
  ignored after switching conversations.
- **Native notifications:** meeting reminders, saved recordings, completed
  summaries, and service issues use the Yulu App identity and concise messages.
  Clicking a notification opens the matching recording or Settings. Foreground
  notices use a lightweight toast, and duplicates are suppressed. A summary
  notification appears only after its artifacts have been committed.
- **Meeting prompts:** native dialogs explain when a scheduled meeting has
  ended. Continue Recording is the default; Escape, timeout, unavailable UI, or
  an older prompt cannot stop a newer recording.

## Updating

Use Yulu's update check, or download `yulu-macos-arm64-v0.25.0.dmg`, open it, and
drag `Yulu.app` into `/Applications` after recording has finished. Configuration,
recordings, provider selections, and credentials remain outside the App.

Automatic insertion requires macOS Accessibility permission for **Yulu** itself;
permission for YuluCapture or an older StatusAgent does not grant it to Yulu.
System banner delivery also depends on Yulu's notification permission and macOS
Focus settings. The update does not change these system permissions.

## Verification scope

Regression coverage includes audio resampling and realtime lifecycle, text
preservation, conversation scope and streaming, shortcut gestures, native panel
layout in both appearances, permission denial, notification dispatch, and safe
meeting-stop behavior. The release pipeline runs Python and TypeScript checks,
native compilation, signing, notarization, runtime inventory, public asset
checksums, and signed update-feed validation.

Native layout and prompt controls were exercised on a physical Mac. Automated
checks do not establish successful insertion in every destination app, real
meeting notification delivery, or the complete fresh-install and cross-version
migration matrix. macOS 13 remains the deployment target.
