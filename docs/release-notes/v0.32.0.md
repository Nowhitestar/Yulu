# Yulu v0.32.0

Live notes work again, the menu bar shows your calendar, and Yulu can open when
you log in.

## Live notes fixed

In v0.30.0 and v0.31.0, every live notes update failed before reaching xAI,
so the panel only ever said **The last update didn't come through. Yulu tries
again with the next update.** The Host gave live notes a 45-second request
window, but the xAI text client allowed at most the 20-second dictation window
and rejected the request on your Mac. No meeting text was sent.

Live notes now have their own 45-second window, so key points, tables and
diagrams build up during the meeting again. Nothing else about live notes
changes: they still need the data notice accepted once and a connected xAI
account, audio is never sent, and they never switch to another model or
provider. When an update does fail, the Host now prints a short reason, such as
an HTTP status or a timeout and never meeting text, to its standard error.

## Your calendar in the menu bar

- **Next meetings in the menu.** The menu now starts with your calendar: the
  meeting under way or starting within the hour, with how long until it ends or
  starts, **Join Google Meet Meeting** (or Zoom, Teams, Tencent Meeting, Feishu,
  Webex, DingTalk) and **Record This Meeting**; then the rest of today and the
  next two days that have meetings. Each meeting's submenu shows its time, its
  calendar and the join link. With macOS calendars, each meeting carries its
  calendar's colour.
- **The next meeting beside the icon.** From 30 minutes before a meeting until
  it ends, the menu bar shows its title and how soon it starts, for example
  `Design review · in 12 min`, then `· now`. Long titles are shortened so it
  never takes more than a set width. Change the lead time, or turn it off, in
  **Settings → General → Show the next meeting in the menu bar**.
- **A shorter menu.** Start Dictation, Translate to English and Recent
  Recordings left the menu. Dictation and translation keep their shortcuts, and
  recordings are in Yulu's window.

The calendar comes from the calendars Yulu already reads for meeting reminders;
nothing new is connected.

## Open at login

**Settings → General → Open at login** adds Yulu to macOS login items. After
you log in, Yulu waits in the menu bar with its window loaded in the background;
click the Dock icon or open Yulu from the menu bar to see it. The switch shows
what **System Settings → General → Login Items** says, and either place can turn
it off. It is off until you turn it on. If macOS asks you to approve Yulu, the
row opens Login Items for you.

## Updating

Use Yulu's update check, or download `yulu-macos-arm64-v0.32.0.dmg` and drag
`Yulu.app` into Applications after recording has finished. If macOS blocks the
first launch, confirm the download came from the official release page, then
use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
