# Yulu v0.31.0

A new identity, and live notes on by default.

## A new mark

Yulu's mark is now one stroke: three swells, each lower than the last, are
speech; the stroke settles into a straight line, the written record; and a short
blue segment after a gap is the line being written now. It replaces the blue
glass lens everywhere Yulu shows itself.

- **App icon:** a quiet white tile with the mark, with simplified drawings at
  16 and 32 px so the icon stays sharp in Finder lists and the Dock's smallest
  sizes.
- **Menu bar:** the three states now differ by shape, not only color. Idle shows
  the mark with its cursor and stays still. Recording turns the cursor into a
  steady red dot, and the swells rise and fall with the sound of the meeting.
  Processing turns the cursor into three dots that light in turn.
- **Live notes panel:** collapsed, the panel becomes the app icon, and its mark
  keeps writing while you record: the swells follow the sound and the blue
  cursor writes, holds and erases.
- **Reduce Motion:** with **System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Reduce
  motion** on, every icon stays still.
- **Web UI and README:** the browser tab icon follows light and dark mode, and
  the README header and social card use the new mark.

The full brand kit — the mark, lockups, app icon, menu bar icons, favicons,
social card, colors and motion references, with the script that regenerates
them — is in [`assets/brand/`](../../assets/brand/README.md).

## Live notes on by default

Live notes are now switched on out of the box (ADR-013), but nothing is sent
until you confirm once what they send. **Settings → Recording & summaries →
Live notes** shows the notice right away with **Accept and start** and **Turn
off live notes**, and the panel tells you live notes haven't started yet and
which step is missing. After you accept, new transcript text, including what
other people say, your notes, and matching glossary terms go directly to xAI
with response storage off. Audio is never sent for live notes. They still need
a connected xAI account and never switch to another model or provider. If your
settings already say live notes are off, they stay off; your own notes are
saved on this Mac either way.

## Updating

Use Yulu's update check, or download `yulu-macos-arm64-v0.31.0.dmg` and drag
`Yulu.app` into Applications after recording has finished. If macOS blocks the
first launch, confirm the download came from the official release page, then
use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. The Dock and Finder
may keep showing the old icon until macOS refreshes its icon cache; logging out
and back in refreshes it.
