# Preserve Core Activation as evidence

Yulu records Core Activation Evidence linked to the qualifying recording rather
than storing a naked completion boolean or continuously deriving activation from
the recordings that still exist. Existing users may be bootstrapped from a
qualifying historical recording only when its artifacts and provider provenance
can be verified; after evidence is recorded, deleting that recording or changing
providers does not revoke the milestone. This preserves an auditable reason for
activation without repeatedly onboarding users who clean up their library, while
current capability health remains a separate concern.
